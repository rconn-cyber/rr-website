About the Board

The Executive Board of Rough Riders, Inc. is elected annually and serves as the governing body of the 1st U.S. Volunteer Cavalry Regiment — Tampa Rough Riders. Board members uphold the organization's 501(c)(3) nonprofit charter and its mission to preserve the legacy of Theodore Roosevelt, the Spanish-American War, and the men who trained and departed from Tampa in 1898.

The Board oversees museum operations at 601 N. 19th St., member services, annual fundraising events, community partnerships, and the preservation of the Teddy Roosevelt Museum and Library collection.

For inquiries, contact the organization at office@tamparoughriders.org or call 813-248-1898.
